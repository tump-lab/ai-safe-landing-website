Why people matter

Safe AI starts with understanding people.

AI should adapt to people, not the other way around.

  1. People are different.

    Each person has their own needs and preferences.

  2. People change.

    Needs shift with time and context.

  3. People need trust.

    Privacy and choice come first.

How we work

Validate AI before it reaches people.

User Modeling

Learn each person’s needs, preferences, and context.

User Simulation

Use user proxies to collect early feedback before real users are involved.

Trustworthy Evaluation

Check that AI is fair, explains itself, avoids one-sided suggestions, and protects privacy.
